# Session Context — 2026-06-13 (10 days to competition)

Working branch: `austin`. Goal of the session: fix shooting (slow + far-distance
inaccuracy), fix turret aim drift, tune velocities, and build out the 24-ball
close auto (`CloseAuto24`). Below is what was diagnosed, what changed, and what
still needs to be verified on the field.

---

## 1. Diagnoses (root causes found)

- **Slow + inaccurate shooting** → the flywheel is a **bang-bang** controller
  (`Shooter.periodic()`, full-power-or-off + FLOAT coast). It oscillates around
  target instead of holding it, so it's slow to satisfy the "at speed" gate and
  the exit velocity is inconsistent (worst at far range). A closed-loop velocity
  PID would fix all of this but the team is keeping bang-bang for now.
- **Random turret drift** → suspected **voltage sag** from the always-on flywheel
  (bang-bang full-power pulses) browning out the **goBILDA Pinpoint**, corrupting
  heading → random turret directions. Worse at far shots (higher standby RPM).
- **Deterministic 3–4° turret error at one heading** → turret PIDF is **P-only**
  (`Robot.java`, `25,0,0,0`); P-only can't hold against a position-dependent
  disturbance (cable harness drag). Fix path: bump P + relieve cable, or custom
  PID/feedforward.
- **Far overshoot/undershoot** → RPM lookup table values; far profile also used to
  clamp past 133.5". Now extended/retuned.

## 3. CloseAuto24 — current design

- **Hood + velocity constant** the whole auto (set once at start, never changed).
- **Intake always ON** in one direction (`INTAKE_ON = -1.0`). The **blocker
  (magazine cover) solely gates shooting** — `burst()` just opens → settles →
  waits → closes; the always-on intake feeds through the open blocker. Cover
  values were swapped (servo was inverted): `COVER_OPEN = 1.0`, `COVER_CLOSE = 0.1`.
  Blocker is closed at INIT (during the push).
- **Turret auto-aims** at the shoot spot (`shootTurretDeg()`), so `SHOOT_HEADING_DEG`
  is a free knob (set to 30° for a smoother gate transition). Fine correction:
  `SHOOT_TURRET_OFFSET_DEG` (try ≈ −2 to match old hand-tuned aim).
- **Push-to-start**: robot is initialized at `(48, 120, INIT_HEADING_DEG=90)`,
  PUSHED to the real start during INIT (odometry tracks it; live pose on telemetry),
  and `toShoot0` builds from the **actual pushed pose**. Pick alliance BEFORE pushing.
- **Rows**: top y=84, middle y=60, bottom y=36, each sweeping **x = 36 → 18**.
- **Gate**: intake at `(9.5, 61)` heading **330°**; approach to `(GATE_APPROACH_X=14, 61)`
  then drive **straight in along −x**. 2-second wait at the gate (`GATE_WAIT_MS`).
- **Shoot spot**: `(52, 94)` — moved +x to clear the top ball line on to/from paths.
